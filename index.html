ORLAND

Privacy Policy and Safety

Effective November 11, 2026

ORLAND is a relaxing 3D island where you talk with AI companions. This page explains what data ORLAND uses, where it goes, and how we keep conversations safe.

ORLAND is for adults 18 and older.

The companions are artificial intelligence, not real people.

ORLAND is run by Jhoan Jaimes Lozano, based in Bellevue, Washington, United States. In this policy, “we” means the person or business running ORLAND.

What we collect and where it lives

Most of your ORLAND data stays on your iPhone. Our server keeps only what it needs to run your account.

DataWhere it’s keptWhy
Email address and passwordOur account server (Supabase); passwords are stored in encrypted formTo sign you in and reset your password
Daily message countOur account serverTo apply the daily conversation limit
Confirmation that you’re 18 or olderNot stored; you confirm it when you sign upTo keep ORLAND adults-only
Conversations, companion memories, and island changesYour iPhoneTo keep your island and conversations yours
App settingsYour iPhoneTo remember your preferences

Voice input. If you use the microphone, your speech is turned into text using Apple’s speech recognition. On recent iPhones this happens on the device. On older models, Apple’s speech service may be used.

We don’t use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.

How the AI companions work

When you message a companion, your message and some context go to Anthropic’s Claude AI to create the reply. Nobody at ORLAND reads your conversations.

  • What is sent: your message, recent conversation, and the companion’s notes about you that the reply needs. It travels through our server, which doesn’t store it.
  • Two steps per message: a quick safety check, then the companion’s reply.
  • Your consent: before your first conversation, ORLAND asks for your permission to send messages to Anthropic. You can review this choice in Settings.
  • Training: under Anthropic’s commercial terms, your conversations aren’t used to train its AI models. Anthropic may keep data briefly for safety and abuse monitoring, as described in Anthropic’s privacy policy.
  • Mistakes: AI can be wrong. Don’t rely on the companions for medical, legal, financial, or other professional advice.

Your choices and rights

We never sell or share your personal information, and ORLAND has no ads.

  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account. This removes your account and server data right away. Data on your iPhone is removed when you delete the app.
  • See what we store: the in-app privacy page shows what leaves your phone. You can also email us for a copy of your account data.
  • How long we keep data: your account data stays until you delete your account. Daily message counts are used only for that day’s limit and are deleted with your account.
  • Washington, California, and other privacy laws: you can ask to know, correct, or delete your personal information, and we won’t treat you differently for asking. Email us at jhoancreate@icloud.com.

Safety

ORLAND’s companions are AI, not people, and ORLAND is not a replacement for professional help. The app reminds you of this at the start of each conversation.

  • Adults only: ORLAND is for people 18 and older and may not be suitable for minors. You confirm your age when you sign up.
  • Crisis support: every message goes through a safety check on your phone and an AI safety check. If a message suggests someone may be in danger, such as thoughts of suicide or self-harm, the companion stops normal conversation and shows crisis resources, including the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline in the US and hotlines for other regions.
  • Always available: the “Need help now?” button in every chat opens crisis resources at any time, even without an internet connection or after the daily limit.
  • Boundaries: companions don’t present themselves as doctors, therapists, or other professionals, and are designed to encourage real-world support.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911 or your local emergency number.
